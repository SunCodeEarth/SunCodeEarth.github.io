Transforming Contiguous United States Counties by Census Population

Effortless Animation of over 3,000 Polygons

Cartogram

In a cartogram, each polygon is resized proportionally to a variable, in this case the population of a county.

Created by Shipeng Sun
Associate Professor

Department of Georgraphy and Environmental Science
Hunter College

Data produced with Carto3F, an algorithm developed by Dr. Sun.

Visualization is based on d3.js
and WebGL.

OpenAI Codex (GPT 6.1 Sol Light) was used to refine and improve the WebGL code.
